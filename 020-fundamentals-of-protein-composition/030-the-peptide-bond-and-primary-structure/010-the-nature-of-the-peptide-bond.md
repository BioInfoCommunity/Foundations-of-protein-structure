# The nature of the peptide bond

Although the peptide bond itself is rigid due to partial double-bond character within the amide plane, the polypeptide backbone contains two additional bonds that are free to rotate. These rotations give rise to torsion (dihedral) angles that define the overall conformation of the protein backbone:

* The **φ (phi)** angle corresponds to rotation around the N-Cα bond at the start of each residue.
* The **ψ (psi)** angle corresponds to rotation around the Cα-C bond at the end of each residue.

The trans form is the more favoured state because in this arrangement repulsion between non-bonded atoms connected to the Cα centre are minimised.
