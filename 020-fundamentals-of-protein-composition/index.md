# Fundamentals of protein composition

Proteins perform most of the work inside cells. In this section, we focus on how their three-dimensional structure arises from genetic information and why their structure is central to their function.

Proteins achieve their functional diversity by interacting with a wide range of biological partners, including other proteins, nucleic acids such as DNA and RNA, lipids, and small molecules. In this module, we will explore the fundamentals of protein structure with a particular focus on the 3D shape that a functional protein adopts.

By the end of this section, you will be able to:

* Describe how information flows from DNA to RNA to protein, and how this determines protein structure and function.
* Describe and compare the chemical properties of amino acid side chains and examine how these properties influence protein folding.
* Explain how peptide bonds are formed and differentiate between rigid and flexible components of the polypeptide backbone.
